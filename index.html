How it Works

Simple steps to get your things delivered.

Post a Request

Need food from the cafeteria or medicine from the gate? Just post a request with your location and a small reward.

Accepted by Peer

A student nearby who is already at that location accepts your request. No professional delivery guys, just fellow students.

Safe Delivery

Receive your items at your doorstep (hall/department) and pay the reward. Build your reputation in the community.
